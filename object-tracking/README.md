### Object Tracking

This practical introduces object tracking as the problem of
maintaining object identity over time as objects and cameras move, a
core capability in many real-world vision systems such as autonomous
driving, robotics, video surveillance, and human-computer interaction.
Tracking requires detecting objects in each frame and associating them
across time while they undergo motion, interact with other objects,
enter or leave the field of view, and experience temporary occlusion.
